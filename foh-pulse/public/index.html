Front of house wages vs FOH sales, right now

Front of house only: sales are Square items in your FOH categories (after discounts, GST taken out) and wages are rostered shifts at your FOH locations. Hours come from the published roster, not actual clock-in/out, so this assumes everyone works their rostered shift as planned. Refunds aren't deducted. These are live Square figures, not the Revenue on the main Dashboard (that comes from Xero), so they won't match exactly. Projected end-of-day numbers are a rough straight-line estimate, not a forecast model.
